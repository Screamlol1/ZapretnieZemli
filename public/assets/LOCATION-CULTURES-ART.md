# Иллюстрации культурных наборов

Созданы встроенным image_gen, прозрачные оригинальные PNG. Официальные иллюстрации книг не копировались. Каждый PNG — атлас 18 объектов. Кадры, названия и рекомендуемые размеры находятся в `location-cultures.json`; обрезка выполняется SVG при отображении, исходные PNG сохранены без обработки.

- `/assets/location-cultures-a-v1.png`: люди, эльфы, полуэльфы, дварфы, полурослики, полуволки.
- `/assets/location-cultures-b-v1.png`: орки, свартальвы, огры, ящеролюды, Whiners, аслены.

## Финальные запросы

### Атлас A

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a production sprite atlas for a dark medieval fantasy tabletop location map. Create a TRANSPARENT PNG atlas, 1024 wide x 1536 tall, exactly THREE equal columns by SIX equal rows (18 separate sprites). Each sprite entirely inside its cell with 20px empty transparent padding; no touching, no shadows outside cell, no text, no labels, no grid, no background. Consistent high overhead 3/4 map view, hand painted muted moss/ochre/slate palette, fine dark ink outlines, realistic rough medieval materials, simple readable silhouettes matching a parchment RPG map. No people. Row1 HUMANS: timber thatched cottage / timber trading hall / small stone watchtower. Row2 ELVES: elegant green leaf-roof woodland dwelling / curved living-wood open meeting pavilion / ancient tree enclosing a small ruby shrine. Row3 HALF-ELVES: timber-and-pale-stone forest cottage / herbalist workshop with herb beds / small pale-stone garden observatory. Row4 DWARVES: heavy angular slate-and-granite dwelling / stone forge workshop / squat massive granite gatehouse. Row5 HALFLINGS: low turf-roof cottage / small convivial thatched tavern / terraced vegetable garden with low fence. Row6 WOLFKIN: hide lean-to shelter / communal timber-and-hide hunting shelter / circle of worn standing stones and drums. Architecture varied in silhouette, never simply recolors. Individual buildings only, no scenic square bases. Each cell independent, no connectors. All sprites consistent camera and light from upper left. Transparent empty canvas around each sprite.

### Атлас B

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite atlas for a dark medieval fantasy tabletop location map. TRANSPARENT PNG 1024 wide x 1536 tall, exactly THREE equal columns by SIX equal rows, 18 isolated sprites. Every object fits its cell, with 20px transparent padding, no crossing row or column boundaries. No text, labels, grid or background. Consistent high overhead 3/4 view, hand painted muted moss/ochre/slate palette, fine dark ink outlines, rough medieval wood stone hide reed textures, believable construction, no characters, no square scenic bases. Light upper left. Row1 ORCS: rough timber longhouse / sturdy timber barracks / timber watchtower with purple pennant. Row2 GOBLINS: low portable hide tent / covered wolf pen of branches / small moon ritual circle with wooden posts. Row3 OGRES: oversized crude log hut / boulder den entrance / large open firepit with logs and crude drums. Row4 SAURIANS: reed hut on timber stilts with a small dry wooden deck / raised wooden trading platform with reed canopy / bundle-reed fish traps and short waterside wooden jetty without water around it. Row5 WHINERS small subterranean kin: porous honeycombed limestone mound with tiny black openings / cutaway small chamber inside porous limestone / cluster of low tunnel mouths in porous grey stone. Row6 ASLENE HUMAN RIDERS: earth-red round travelling tent / long timber stable with earth-red saddlecloth / carved wooden horse-head hitching posts around small campfire. Only architecture or terrain objects, each cell independent. This set must have varied silhouettes and credible scale relationships. All surroundings empty transparent, no drop shadow outside each cell.
